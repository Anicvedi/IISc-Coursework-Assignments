## ?? Laboratory Modules & Semiconductor Fabrication:
1. **Cleanroom Protocols & Safety:** Class 100/1000 cleanroom operating standards.
2. **Thermal Oxidation & Wet Chemical Cleaning:** RCA cleaning (SC-1, SC-2), high-temperature dry and wet silicon dioxide growth ($), ellipsometry thickness measurement.
3. **Photolithography:** Spin coating photoresist, UV mask alignment, exposure, and chemical development.
4. **Thin Film Deposition:** Thermal evaporation, DC/RF Magnetron sputtering, and Plasma Enhanced Chemical Vapor Deposition (PECVD).
5. **Dry & Wet Etching:** Reactive Ion Etching (RIE) and buffered oxide etching (BOE).
6. **Device Characterization:** Four-point probe sheet resistance measurement, CV profiling, and semiconductor parameter analyzer IV measurements.
